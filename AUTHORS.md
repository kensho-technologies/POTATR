## Acknowledgments

POTATR builds on the following open-source projects:

- [Table Transformer](https://github.com/microsoft/table-transformer) (Microsoft, MIT License):
  dataset loading, table post-processing, evaluation metrics and the training entry point.
- [LOBSTR](https://github.com/microsoft/LOBSTR) (Microsoft, MIT License): the graph model and
  Hungarian matcher that POTATR extends.
- [DETR](https://github.com/facebookresearch/detr) (Facebook, Apache License 2.0): the backbone,
  transformer and training utilities.
- [PubTabNet](https://github.com/ibm-aur-nlp/PubTabNet) (IBM, Apache License 2.0): the TEDS metric.
